About

Engineering interfaces with the same discipline as a machined part.

This portfolio belongs to Al Shahriar Sayon, a Computer Science student who builds software the way I'd want to use it — quiet, precise, and considered down to the pixel. My work sits at the intersection of frontend engineering, AI tooling, and interface craft.

Outside of shipping product, I spend time in prompt engineering, modern UI systems, and the small details most people build to move past instead of through.
